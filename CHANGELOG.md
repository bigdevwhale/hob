## [1.0.1]

- Rooms can be put in your own order: hold a room on the home screen and drag it to its new place (#1).
- Places can be renamed and deleted: tap the chosen place again, or hold any place. A place goes only when its rooms hold no things.
- Settings → StillCraft has a line to support Hob.
- Fixed: sheets opened from the home and hob screens (another place, selling) no longer hide their buttons under the tab bar.

Адреса можно переименовать и удалить: нажмите на выбранный адрес ещё раз или зажмите любой.

## [1.0.0]

The first version.

- Your own house: places, rooms, places to keep things and numbered boxes; rooms stay dark until something in them is written down.
- Search by name, room, place, box label or number; kinds of things, built-in and your own. The sign and the kind are guessed from the name.
- The hob's notes and a morning reminder: things needed soon, lent things overdue, borrowed things to give back, what a child has grown into.
- Children's things to grow into: sizes from the label, a growth forecast from each child's measurements, who wore a thing and who comes next.
- Lending with reminders, things borrowed from others, a thing's history.
- Clear-out of things untouched for a year: keep, give away, sell or throw out.
- Cosiness, the hob's titles and signs; what has gone on and what it brought in.
- Evening and day themes, a copy of the whole house in one file, CSV. No internet permission.
- English and Russian.
